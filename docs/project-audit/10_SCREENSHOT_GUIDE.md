# Screenshot Guide

*Since actual visual screenshots cannot be provided, this document describes the exact visual experience of navigating the site.*

## Homepage (`/`)
- **Above the fold:** The screen is dominated by a massive, edge-to-edge Hero image. The navigation bar sits transparently at the top. The words "Biruh Adorn" are barely visible until scrolling begins.
- **Scrolling down:** The header background turns solid dark navy. A delicate brand introduction appears in large Bodoni text ("Material, Movement, Form").
- **Middle page:** You encounter the "Featured Works". Two large, asymmetrical images take up the screen. One is a 3:4 portrait, the other a 4:3 landscape, breaking the standard grid to feel editorial.
- **Lower page:** A grid of Instagram photos appears, followed by a massive, sparse section simply stating "Empowering Jewelry" with a button to "Explore Jewelry".

## Gallery (`/jewelry`)
- **Above the fold:** The page starts with a clean, centered title "Gallery" and a short description. Immediately below is the filter bar. There is a search input with a magnifying glass icon, and two rows of delicate, uppercase text buttons ("ALL", "RINGS", "NECKLACES" and "ALL COLLECTIONS", "AURORA", "STRATUM").
- **Scrolling down:** The screen fills with a strict 4-column grid of jewelry photography. Each photo is perfectly rectangular (3:4 ratio). Below each photo is the name of the piece and the price.
- **Hovering:** When you hover over a photo, it slightly zooms in, and a dark overlay appears with a white border box reading "VIEW DETAILS".
- **Clicking:** The screen darkens heavily (glassmorphism blur). A large modal slides up from the bottom. On the left is a massive square image of the jewelry. On the right, the title, price, and description. At the very bottom of the modal is a bright WhatsApp-green button with a chat icon.

## Custom Consultation (`/custom`)
- **Above the fold:** A sophisticated header explaining the bespoke process.
- **Scrolling down:** The page splits perfectly down the middle. 
- **Left side (Guide):** A list of instructions ("1. Perspective", "2. Details") explaining what the client should prepare.
- **Right side (Form):** A series of dark, minimalist input lines. Instead of traditional boxed inputs, they are just single bottom-borders that glow gold when clicked. The user types their name, selects a jewelry type from a dropdown, and types their idea.
- **Bottom:** A "Start Consultation on WhatsApp" button that changes color when hovered.

## About (`/about`)
- **Above the fold:** An editorial photo alongside the title "Our Story".
- **Scrolling down:** The layout is intentionally chaotic but controlled. A paragraph of text on the left, empty space on the right. Then an image on the right, empty space on the left.
- **Visual Moment:** The "Fashion to Jewelry" section. The words "Perspective -> Fashion", "Structure -> Form", "Expression -> Movement" cascade down the screen, connected by small arrows, culminating in the massive word "Jewelry".

## Contact (`/contact`)
- **Above the fold:** A very calm, centered layout. The header "Contact Us". Below it, three columns arranged horizontally: WhatsApp number, Email address, and Locations. 
- **Bottom:** A single, prominent button to message on WhatsApp. The entire page fits on one screen without needing to scroll.
