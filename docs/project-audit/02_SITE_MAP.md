# Site Map

## `/` (Homepage)
- **Purpose:** Luxury editorial introduction to the brand.
- **Main sections:** 
  - Hero
  - Brand Introduction
  - Featured Jewelry
  - Craftsmanship
  - Empowering Jewelry
  - Collections Preview
  - Custom Jewelry
  - Founder
  - Services Preview
  - Testimonials Preview
  - Instagram Preview
  - Social Preview
  - Final CTA
- **Primary CTA:** "Explore Jewelry" / View specific collections.
- **Secondary CTA:** "Talk to Biruh Adorn" / Custom Consultation.
- **Conversion goal:** Guide users into the gallery or to inquire about bespoke pieces.
- **Current completion status:** COMPLETE (Awaiting final assets).

## `/jewelry` (Gallery)
- **Purpose:** Unified catalog of all available pieces.
- **Main sections:**
  - Header (Title & Description)
  - Interactive Filter Bar (Search, Category, Collection)
  - 4-Column Product Grid
  - Product Detail Modal (Image gallery, details, WhatsApp CTA)
- **Primary CTA:** "Order on WhatsApp" (inside Product Modal).
- **Secondary CTA:** Filtering and exploring.
- **Conversion goal:** Product-specific WhatsApp inquiry.
- **Current completion status:** COMPLETE (Awaiting final catalog data).

## `/jewelry/[slug]` (Product Detail Page)
- **Purpose:** Deep linkable, SEO-friendly page for an individual product.
- **Main sections:** (Currently visually replaced by the Gallery Modal for standard flow, but the route exists for direct access).
- **Primary CTA:** "Inquire on WhatsApp".
- **Conversion goal:** Product-specific WhatsApp inquiry.
- **Current completion status:** PARTIAL (Architecture exists, but primary user flow uses the modal on the `/jewelry` page).

## `/custom` (Custom Jewelry)
- **Purpose:** Detail the bespoke creation process and solicit consultations.
- **Main sections:**
  - Custom Hero
  - Custom Process (Step-by-step)
  - Inspiration Guide
  - Consultation Form (WhatsApp generator)
  - Custom CTA
- **Primary CTA:** "Start Consultation" (via WhatsApp).
- **Secondary CTA:** N/A.
- **Conversion goal:** High-intent bespoke jewelry inquiry.
- **Current completion status:** COMPLETE.

## `/about` (Our Story)
- **Purpose:** Communicate the brand narrative, founder story, and artistic philosophy.
- **Main sections:**
  - About Hero
  - Founder Story
  - Fashion To Jewelry (Conceptual sequence)
  - Material Story
  - Material Movement Form
  - Empowering Jewelry
  - Craftsmanship Story
  - About CTA
- **Primary CTA:** "Explore the Gallery" / "Commission a Custom Piece".
- **Secondary CTA:** N/A.
- **Conversion goal:** Brand affinity and progression to product exploration.
- **Current completion status:** COMPLETE.

## `/contact` (Contact)
- **Purpose:** Direct communication channels.
- **Main sections:**
  - Header
  - Contact Grid (WhatsApp, Email, Locations)
  - Primary Action Button
- **Primary CTA:** "Message on WhatsApp".
- **Secondary CTA:** Email link.
- **Conversion goal:** General inquiry.
- **Current completion status:** COMPLETE.

*(Note: The `/collections` and `/collections/[slug]` routes were explicitly removed from the architecture to streamline the user journey into a single `/jewelry` gallery experience.)*
